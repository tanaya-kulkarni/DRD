The world we want

    The World Economic Forum describes a "flourishing world" as one in which sustainable economic development does not leave nature behind, in which individual gain and collective societal progress are mutually reinforcing, in which freedom, equity and human rights are undisputable principles, and in which governments facilitate, convene and empower other stakeholders to shape the future.

    What stands in our way?

    Today, profit often takes precedence over people, powerful institutions escape accountability, and economic growth continues to strain our planet. Digital products use dark patterns to influence our choices, while opaque systems, unequal opportunities, and barriers to access leave many people behind.

    As consumption grows and human autonomy is compromised, the progress we celebrate often comes with costs that remain unseen or unequally shared.

    The Investigation

    For us to understand how digital products surface potential dark patterns and unethical practices, we took help of Phonepe. As part of our investigation, we looked through its terms and conditions, app interfaces, customer reviews as well as company documents.

    We were able to understand Phonepe's role as a payment aggregator, and therefore its ethical responsibilities. By comparing legal terms, design decisions and business objectives, we understood Phonepe's goals as a company.
